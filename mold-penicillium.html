Penicillium

Penicillium is a genus of mold commonly found in soil, decaying vegetation, and indoor environments. It appears as blue-green fuzzy colonies.

Some species are beneficial, such as those used to produce antibiotics and cheese, but others can spoil food and produce mycotoxins harmful to humans.

Prevention

• Store food in dry and cool conditions • Improve air circulation • Avoid prolonged moisture exposure • Clean contaminated surfaces immediately
