Aspergillus

Aspergillus is a genus of common molds found in soil, air, decaying plants, stored grains, and indoor dust. It spreads through tiny airborne spores.

Some species, such as Aspergillus fumigatus, can cause lung infections, while Aspergillus flavus may produce aflatoxin, a toxic substance that can contaminate food and harm the liver.

Prevention

• Maintain humidity below 60% • Improve ventilation in storage areas • Keep drying environments clean and dry • Store grains and nuts in dry conditions
